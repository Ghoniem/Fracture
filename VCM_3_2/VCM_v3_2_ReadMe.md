# VCM v3.2: Final SIF Verifications and Crack Growth Algorithm Fixes

The objectives of this version are two fold: 

(1) finalize the verifications of the Stress Intensity Factor (SIF) for a curved crack, a polyline representation of the curved crack, and the branched crack.

(2) Check on the crack growth algorthim to make sure that it is stable and assess any differences between the max hoop stress theory and the min elastic energy theory as a basis for crack tip advance.

(3) Compare the numerical simulations to the experiments of Shetty et al and to our experiments with Sheng and Alabdullah
